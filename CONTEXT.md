# Yelling At Robots

The context for positioning and describing the Yelling At Robots technology podcast.

## Identity

**Canonical site**: `yellingatrobots.com` is the public, canonical home of Yelling At Robots.
`robot.gripe`, `oldmanyellsatrobots.com`, and `yellingatclankers.com` are aliases that redirect to
it. _Avoid_: Treating aliases as equivalent canonical sites

## Show

**Yelling At Robots**: An entertaining technology podcast where experienced engineers and leaders
make sense of AI-augmented software engineering and the broader transition into an AI-shaped world.
_Avoid_: Generic AI news show, AI tutorial series

**AI transition**: The practical and human shift toward AI-assisted software development,
engineering leadership, and technology work. _Avoid_: AI revolution, future of AI

**Sane AI**: Practical, skeptical, grounded ways to use AI without hype or panic. _Avoid_: AI
evangelism, AI fearmongering

**AI-anxious audience**: Technical and non-technical people trying to understand and navigate the
uncertainty, anxiety, and opportunity created by AI. _Avoid_: Everyone, normies

**Show voice**: Experienced, candid, practical, irreverent, deliberately funny, and accessible to
non-specialists. _Avoid_: Corporate, academic, alarmist

## Web presence

**Episode page**: A crawlable canonical page for one podcast episode, using the existing Yelling At
Robots visual language while exposing episode context to search engines and non-JavaScript visitors.
_Avoid_: Treating the client-rendered homepage feed as the only episode representation

## Content

**News and developments**: Commentary on recent technology and AI events through the hosts'
engineering and leadership experience.

**Coding episodes**: Episodes that demonstrate or examine software development with AI assistance.

**Perspective episodes**: Conversations about using AI responsibly and sanely in real engineering
and technology work, explained so non-specialists can understand why the news matters and how much
they should care.

**Transcript**: The spoken text of an episode, provided as a convenience for readers and for people
who prefer reading to listening. Automatically generated transcripts may contain errors or unedited
asides.
